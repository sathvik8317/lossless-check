How it works

MP3 and AAC encoders throw away the top of the spectrum: about 16 kHz and up at 128 kbps, 19 kHz at 192, 20 kHz at 320. Converting that file to FLAC keeps the hole. This page averages the spectrum of each song and looks for a sharp "wall" onto a flat noise floor. Real recordings keep sloping and wobbling up to the format's own limit.

Walls at 20.4 kHz or higher are not flagged, because many genuine masters have one there. Very high-bitrate VBR MP3 (LAME V0) and AAC 256 leave no wall, so they can't be told apart, but those already sound close to lossless. Click any row to see its spectrum.
